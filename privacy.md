Privacy Policy for Groove Master 
Last Updated: October 14, 2026 
Kagiso Sepeng operates the Groove Master mobile application for Android. 
We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices 
regarding the information handled when you use our App. 
1. Information Collection and Use 
Groove Master is designed exclusively as a local, pass-the-phone party game. 
• Local Session Data: Player names, genre selections, track titles, and point scores are 
processed and stored strictly within your device's temporary memory (RAM) or local state 
storage. This data never leaves your device and is permanently wiped when a application 
session is closed or reset. 
• No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally 
identifiable information (PII), such as your email address, phone number, location, or device 
contacts. 
2. External Third-Party Links (YouTube Music) 
Our App provides convenient deep links that launch external music search queries directly 
inside the YouTube Music application or web player. 
• Once you click a link to view or play a track, you exit the App and interact with a third-party 
platform. We do not control, track, or have access to any data collected by YouTube Music or 
Google Services. 
• We recommend reviewing the official Google Privacy Policy to understand how they manage 
your search data and cookies on their platforms. 
3. Children's Privacy 
Groove Master is a family-friendly gathering game. Because our App does not harvest or 
request any personal data, accounts, or tracking identifiers from any user, it is fully compliant 
with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global data protection 
guidelines. 
4. Internet Permissions 
The App requires internet access permissions solely to translate your typed text into a web link 
format for external routing. It does not use this connection to transmit background analytics or 
user behaviors to external tracking servers.
